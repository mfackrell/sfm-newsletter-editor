A Story From This Month
Day of Redemption 2026 — when the Body of Christ came together and heaven touched earth

There are moments that leave you speechless… and Day of Redemption 2026 was one of them.

As I looked around throughout the day, I couldn't help but stand in awe of what God was doing.

I saw servant hearts pouring themselves out without hesitation. I saw churches, organizations, businesses, volunteers, and community members standing shoulder to shoulder with one purpose—to love people like Jesus.

I saw friends who are so often overlooked being celebrated, embraced, prayed over, and reminded that they are seen, known, and deeply loved.

I saw baptisms. I saw salvations. I saw people surrendering at the altars. I saw prayers being lifted all across the property. I saw worship that invited the presence of God in such a powerful way that you could feel the Holy Spirit moving everywhere you went.

Every time I turned around, God was writing another story.

What an incredible reminder that the Kingdom isn't built through platforms or programs—it is built through ordinary people saying “yes” to an extraordinary God.

The numbers tell part of the story:
Over 500 people gathered.
More than 80 friends were served meals.
39 haircuts were given.
25 showers were taken.
19 baptisms declared new life in Christ.

But the true impact can't be measured by statistics.

The conversations. The tears. The hugs. The prayers. The worship. The restoration. The hope. The community.

Those moments are Kingdom currency, and their value is priceless and immeasurable.

To every volunteer, worship leader, artist, pastor, prayer team, vendor, sponsor, community partner, church, and every person who simply showed up with a willing heart, we say THANK YOU.

You helped create a place where people encountered the love and hope of Jesus in tangible ways.

We are overwhelmed with gratitude and even more expectant for what God will continue to do. If this day proved anything, it's that when the Body of Christ comes together in unity, heaven touches earth.

To God alone be the glory. ❤️
