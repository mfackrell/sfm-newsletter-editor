A Move of God Among the Streets

When God Builds
What We Could Only Dream

A testimony of vision, obedience, and how God is meeting people right where they are—on the streets, on the beach, and in the places most others overlook.

Key Scripture

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”

Psalm 34:18 (NIV)

Testimony

What began as a late-night prayer in an old coach’s living room has grown into something only God Himself could orchestrate—a full-scale street outreach on the beach designed to love and restore the very people many walk past every day. Redeemed shared the heart behind the Day of Redemption, an event birthed not out of marketing or planning meetings, but through tears, humility, and a simple prayer whispered in faith.

The vision was never “another concert.” From the beginning, God made it clear this was about serving the homeless, the hurting, the addicted, and the forgotten—through practical love and the uncompromised gospel. It includes showers, hot meals, haircuts, fresh clothing, baptisms, foot-washing, testimonies, prayer tents, vendors, and more than twenty-five community resource partners ready to help people step out of survival mode and into hope.

Artists like SYL, Jruth, B Cody, Blake, and the Infiltrate family are traveling in not to perform but to serve—to lift up the name of Jesus and lift up people who feel unseen. With demand for tickets rising and excitement in the community building, Redeemed emphasized that the real heartbeat of the day is simple: show up, say yes, and watch God meet people anywhere, even on a beach pavilion at sunset.

"If you just show up and say yes—even if your yes feels small—God will meet you anywhere." — Redeemed

What God Revealed

God uses simple obedience

The entire event grew from one whispered prayer and a willingness to say yes.

Love must be practical

Showers, clothing, food, and dignity soften hearts for the gospel.

The streets are ready for revival

Many on the margins are waiting for someone to see them, hear them, and believe in them.

God builds what we cannot

Only He could bring together artists, ministries, partners, and provision in this way.

Reflection

God is still moving among the broken, the hurting, and the overlooked. If you feel like you’ve been living on the margins—physically, emotionally, or spiritually—know this: Jesus comes close to the crushed in spirit. You are not disqualified from His love. You are the very one He runs toward. And just like He is doing through this outreach, He wants to meet you right where you are.

Call to Action

Say yes to Jesus today. Whether it’s a prayer, a step toward healing, or a move toward community—take it. God meets us in our yes, no matter how small it feels.
