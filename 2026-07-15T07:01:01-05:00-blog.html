Street Ministry Testimony

When God Moves
On the Streets

A testimony of transformation, provision, and the power of serving the least of these.

Key Scripture

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”

Psalm 34:18 (NIV)

Testimony

This summer, Salty Farm Ministries welcomed mission teams and youth groups who stepped onto the streets of Panama City Beach ready to serve. What they discovered was far more than a trip—it was a holy encounter with people, pain, and the presence of Jesus. Many of the young people arrived nervous, unsure, or hesitant, but through simple acts of kindness, genuine conversations, and the humility of servant leadership, they left changed.

The first half of the year revealed just how powerfully God is moving: 2,150 meals served, 845 showers provided, 1,567 giving-closet visits, more than 80 free haircuts, one baptism, and many individuals connected with housing and essential resources. Nearly everything given away was donated—living proof that God multiplies what His people place in His hands.

One story rose above the rest. A woman trapped in an abusive living situation prayed for a way out. She had found $100 but had no suitcase to carry her belongings to safety. That very night, a donor unexpectedly brought a suitcase—exactly what she needed. No one had coordinated it. No one knew. But God did. This is the heart of the streets: the Lord seeing, knowing, and rescuing His children in real time.

Now the ministry is stepping forward in faith once again. With supplies stored across multiple scattered locations, the need for a centralized warehouse has become critical. A climate-controlled facility with power, restrooms, office space, merchandise storage, and even the potential to receive Feeding the Gulf Coast pallets would expand the ministry’s ability to serve drastically. Monthly partners are needed to make this next step possible, but the team firmly believes—if God calls it, He will fund it.

"When we put what we have in God’s hands, He puts miracles in ours." — Salty Farm Ministries

What God Revealed

God sees every need before we speak it.

The suitcase arriving the same night was not coincidence but divine orchestration.

Service transforms both the giver and the receiver.

Youth teams discovered purpose and compassion through hands-on ministry.

God multiplies generosity.

Thousands were fed, clothed, washed, and encouraged because people gave what they had.

Faith requires movement.

The warehouse dream is a step of obedience, trusting God to provide through His people.

Reflection

Maybe you feel empty-handed today. Maybe you feel broken, tired, or unsure how God could use someone like you. But the same God who provided a suitcase at the perfect moment is the God who sees your needs, hears your cries, and knows your heart. Jesus meets us in the places we feel the most alone and turns ashes into beauty, pain into purpose, and fear into freedom.
